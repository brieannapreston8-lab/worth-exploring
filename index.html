Worth Exploring

Career directions, not answers
An experiment in noticing what work does to you.

A job is made of ordinary days.
Let’s find the work worth getting curious about.

In 10 minutes, we’ll map the patterns in your curiosity, energy, and working style — then turn them into a few directions worth exploring in the real world.

Before you begin

This is an exploration, not an assessment.

Worth Exploring won’t tell you what you should do. You’ll answer 16 questions about what catches your attention, what gives or drains energy, how you like to work, and what matters in your life.

AI looks for patterns across your answers and turns them into career hypotheses, tensions worth noticing, things worth avoiding, and small experiments you can actually try.

A few things worth knowing:

  • No account is required.
  • Your answers are sent to Google’s Gemini AI to generate your report.
  • While Worth Exploring is still being tested, avoid entering confidential or highly sensitive information.
  • The results are hypotheses, not diagnoses or instructions. They may be useful, surprising, incomplete, or wrong.

Keep what makes you curious. Question what doesn’t. Follow what feels worth exploring.
