Privacy, in plain English

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Privacy pages have a habit of making simple things sound mysterious. This one is trying not to. Worth Exploring is built to collect as little personal information as it reasonably can, and to keep your career exploration separate from the product data used to improve the tool.

The short version

  • You answer questions. Those answers go to Google’s Gemini API when you ask for a report.
  • Worth Exploring does not save your questionnaire answers or generated report in its own user database.
  • Gemini request storage/logging is disabled for the Worth Exploring project.
  • PostHog helps us count things like starts, progress, report-generation success, and feedback-link clicks. It does not receive your questionnaire answers or report content.
  • No session replay, form recording, advertising trackers, or behavioural advertising.
  • Vercel hosts the site and handles the technical traffic needed to keep it running.
  • Worth Exploring is for adults 18 and over.

What you tell Worth Exploring

The questionnaire asks about things like your interests, energy, working style, preferences, constraints, and past experiences. The optional final question lets you add context if the neat little boxes missed something important.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, résumé, employer, date of birth, or an account. Please avoid entering confidential or highly sensitive personal information.

Where your answers go

When you click Generate my report, your questionnaire responses — including anything you typed into an open text box — are sent to Google’s paid Gemini API. Gemini looks across those responses and returns the report you asked for.

Google states that prompts and responses submitted through paid Gemini API services are not used to improve Google products. Worth Exploring has request storage/logging disabled for its Gemini project. Google may still process limited information to operate and secure its services, monitor abuse, or meet legal and regulatory requirements.

In other words: Gemini needs your answers to make the report. That is why they are sent there. It is also why we ask you not to include information you would consider confidential or highly sensitive.

What Worth Exploring keeps

Not your questionnaire or report. Worth Exploring does not currently have a user database that stores either one, and it does not build a saved career profile about you.

Your answers stay in the webpage while you complete the questionnaire and view your report. If you leave or reload, Worth Exploring does not have a recovery button waiting in the wings.

A small amount of analytics information is kept separately, described below. It is there to help us understand whether the product works — not to reconstruct what you told it.

What we measure — and what we don’t

Worth Exploring uses PostHog for lightweight product analytics. Right now we care about a handful of fairly ordinary questions: Did someone start? How far did they get? Did the report generate? How long did it take? Did they actually see it? Did they click through to feedback?

To answer those questions, analytics may include a random browser ID that is not your name or email, a temporary session ID, 25/50/75/100% progress milestones, timing information, whether report generation succeeded or failed, a broad error category, device type, referral source, campaign tags in links, and which version of the app/questionnaire/prompt/method was running.

What Worth Exploring does not send to PostHog:

  • your questionnaire answers or anything you type into the questionnaire;
  • your generated report, career hypotheses, or occupation recommendations;
  • your name or email address;
  • health or other sensitive details you may have entered; or
  • session replay, form recordings, or automatically captured page text.

Analytics events pass through Worth Exploring’s own server first, where only an approved list of fields is allowed through. PostHog’s automatic GeoIP location enrichment is turned off, and Worth Exploring does not intentionally send your IP address to PostHog for product analytics.

A random browser ID may stay in local storage for up to 90 days so repeat visits can be counted without an account. A separate session ID lasts for the current browser session. If your browser sends a Do Not Track setting of 1, Worth Exploring does not send product-analytics events or store the persistent analytics ID.

The website plumbing

Worth Exploring is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel processes technical information needed to deliver, secure, diagnose, and operate the site. Its privacy notice says this can include things such as IP address, approximate city/country derived from IP, browser or device information, request information, and service diagnostics.

Worth Exploring does not use that infrastructure information to build a career profile about you.

Feedback is its own thing

At the end of your report, you can choose to open a separate Google Form and tell us what worked, what didn’t, or what made you raise an eyebrow. Feedback is optional.

Your questionnaire answers and generated report are not automatically copied into that form or attached to your feedback. Worth Exploring records that the feedback link was opened, but it cannot currently see whether you completed the external form.

Anything you voluntarily submit through the form may be kept separately for product research and improvement. Worth Exploring will not quietly turn your questionnaire responses into research data simply because you used the tool.

A note about the AI

Generative AI can connect dots. It can also connect the wrong dots, miss a dot entirely, or become far too confident about a dot that was never there.

Worth Exploring is designed for reflection and exploration. It is not a professional career assessment, psychological assessment, diagnosis, or validated prediction of what career you should pursue. The results are hypotheses, not instructions. Keep what makes you curious. Question what doesn’t.

One firm boundary: 18+

Worth Exploring is intended only for adults age 18 and older.
