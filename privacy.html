Privacy, in plain English

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Worth Exploring is designed to collect as little personal information as it reasonably can. There is no account to create, and Worth Exploring does not build or save a personal career profile about you.

The short version

  • Your questionnaire answers are sent through Worth Exploring to Google’s Gemini API so generative AI can create the report you request.
  • Worth Exploring does not save your questionnaire answers or generated report to its own user database.
  • API request storage/logging is disabled for the Worth Exploring Gemini project.
  • Worth Exploring uses PostHog for limited product analytics about how the experience is used. Questionnaire answers and report content are not sent to PostHog.
  • Worth Exploring does not use session replay, form autocapture, advertising trackers, or behavioural advertising.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.
  • Worth Exploring is intended for adults age 18 and older.

What information you provide

Worth Exploring processes the answers you enter into the questionnaire in order to generate your report. Those answers may describe your interests, energy, working style, preferences, constraints, past experiences, or other context you choose to provide.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, résumé, employer, date of birth, or an account in order to use Worth Exploring. Please avoid entering confidential or highly sensitive personal information.

How your report is generated

When you request your report, your questionnaire responses are sent to Google’s Gemini API. Generative AI analyzes the responses together and produces a report containing patterns, tensions worth noticing, possible areas to explore, examples of nearby roles, and small real-world experiments.

Worth Exploring uses your responses for this purpose: to generate the report you requested.

The tool is designed for reflection and exploration. It is not a professional career assessment, psychological assessment, diagnosis, or validated prediction of what career you should pursue.

What Worth Exploring stores

Worth Exploring does not currently have a user database and does not intentionally save your questionnaire answers, generated report, or a personal career profile connected to you.

Your answers remain available in the webpage while you complete the questionnaire and view your report. If you leave or reload the experience, Worth Exploring does not provide a way to recover them.

Limited analytics identifiers are stored separately in your browser as described below. They are used to understand product usage, not to save or reconstruct questionnaire content.

Google Gemini

Worth Exploring uses the paid Google Gemini API to generate reports. Questionnaire responses you submit, including any free-text responses, are processed by Gemini when your report is generated.

Google states that prompts and responses submitted through paid Gemini API services are not used to improve Google products. Worth Exploring has API request storage/logging disabled for its Gemini project. Google may still process limited information as necessary to operate and secure its services, monitor abuse, or meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Because your answers are processed by a third-party AI provider, please do not include information you would consider confidential or highly sensitive.

Product analytics

Worth Exploring uses PostHog to answer a small set of product questions during early testing: whether people start the experience, how far they progress, whether reports generate successfully, how long generation takes, whether reports are viewed, and whether users click through to the separate feedback form.

Analytics events may include a random pseudonymous browser identifier, a temporary session identifier, questionnaire progress milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%), timing information, sanitized generation status/error categories, coarse device category, referral category, UTM campaign tags, and application/questionnaire/prompt/methodology version identifiers.

PostHog does not receive from Worth Exploring:

  • questionnaire answers or free-text responses;
  • generated report content, career hypotheses, or occupation recommendations;
  • your name or email address;
  • health information intentionally entered into the questionnaire; or
  • session replay, form recordings, or automatically captured page text.

Worth Exploring uses a first-party server endpoint to send only approved analytics fields to PostHog. PostHog GeoIP enrichment is disabled, and Worth Exploring does not intentionally send the user’s IP address to PostHog for product analytics.

A random browser identifier may be kept in local storage for up to 90 days so repeat visits can be counted without an account. A separate session identifier is kept in session storage for the current browsing session. If your browser sends a Do Not Track setting of 1, Worth Exploring does not send product-analytics events or store the persistent analytics identifier.

Vercel

Worth Exploring is hosted on Vercel. Vercel processes technical information needed to deliver, secure, diagnose, and operate the website. According to Vercel’s privacy notice, this can include information such as IP address, approximate city/country derived from IP address, browser or device information, request information, and service diagnostics.

Worth Exploring does not use this infrastructure information to build a career profile about you.

Feedback is separate

At the end of a report, you may choose to provide feedback through a Google Form. Providing feedback is optional.

Your questionnaire answers and generated report are not automatically sent to the feedback form or attached to your feedback response. Worth Exploring records only that the feedback link was opened; it cannot currently observe whether the external form was completed.

Information you voluntarily submit through the feedback form may be retained separately for product research and improvement. Worth Exploring will not quietly turn questionnaire responses into research data simply because you used the tool.

About the AI

Worth Exploring uses generative AI to identify patterns and generate possibilities from your responses. Generative AI can misunderstand context, make inaccurate connections, overlook something important, or produce suggestions that simply do not fit.

The results are intended to support reflection and exploration — not to make career decisions for you. Keep what makes you curious. Question what doesn’t.

Age

Worth Exploring is intended only for adults age 18 and older.
